# Company Customer Support Policies and Guidelines

## Return and Refund Policy
Customers may return any product within 30 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in original condition with proof of purchase. Refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Custom or personalized items are non-refundable unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## Shipping and Delivery
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders above $50. Expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout for an additional $12. International shipping is currently supported for over 45 countries, with typical transit times between 7 to 14 business days. Customers will receive a tracking link via email once their order has dispatched.

## Warranty and Repairs
All electronic hardware products include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid immersion, or unauthorized third-party repairs. For extended coverage, customers can purchase our 2-Year Protection Plan within 30 days of product purchase.

## Account and Security
Users can reset their passwords at any time by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is strongly recommended and can be enabled under Account Settings > Security. For account deletion or data export requests, please contact privacy@company.com.

## Customer Support Hours
Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. We are closed on major public holidays. Urgent inquiries submitted outside business hours will be answered on the next business day.
